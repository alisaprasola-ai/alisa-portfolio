Highlights

Shaping the experience

My work spanned new functionality and existing flows. Together with the lead designer, I explored how users would move through each journey, how their decisions would affect the next step, and how the interface should support those interactions.

  • ✧New flows: Turned product functionality into connected screens and interactions.
  • ✧Flow redesign: Reconsidered the structure, sequence, and interface design of existing journeys.
  • ✧Product logic: Collaborated with the lead designer to define how user actions, decisions, and screens connected.
  • ✧Design refinement: Discussed approaches and refined solutions to keep the interface consistent with the agreed flow logic.
Retrospective

Connecting product thinking with interface design

Working on both new and existing flows strengthened my ability to connect interface design with product thinking. It encouraged me to look beyond individual screens and consider how each decision shaped the wider user journey.
